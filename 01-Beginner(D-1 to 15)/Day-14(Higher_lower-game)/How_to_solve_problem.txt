The ways to approach the problem in the programming ;

STEP-1: Breakdown the Problem ;

i) First, think about the problem and then break down a large problem into smaller problems 

ii) This way you can tackle the problem one by one

STEP-2 :Make a TODO list of problems ;

i) Look at the problem make a TODO list, Give the items in the TODO list

ii) Start with easiest : Pick from the TODO list that one that you think is the easiest to begin 

iii) TURN THE PROBLEMS INTO COMMENTS IN YOUR CODE

FOR EXAMPLE : #Import the random module
# Use randint() to generate a random integer

Now : You have taken a big problem --> Broken into smaller problems --> Take the easiest smaller among the smaller problem 
---> Break it down into Comments into Your Code Then

THIS IS THE REAL SKILL OF PROGRAMMERS TAKING SOMETHING THAT'S IMMENSELY COMPLEX AND DIFFICULT , BREAKING IT DOWN INTO SMALLER PROBLEM AND BREAKING IT DOWN FURTHER AND THEN TACKLING THEM FROM THE EASIEST TO THE HARDEST AND DOING THIS ONE BY ONE . EVENTUALLY , BY THE END , YOU WILL END UP WITH YOUR FINAL PRODUCT.

## 🃏 What is Blackjack?

Blackjack is a card game where the goal is to get a score **as close to 21 as possible without going over 21**. You play against the dealer/computer. Each player starts with two cards. Cards from 2–10 are worth their number, while J, Q, and K are worth 10. An Ace is worth **11 or 1**, depending on which value is better for the player. If your first two cards are an Ace and a 10-value card, you have **Blackjack**, which is an immediate winning hand in the basic version of the game.

## 📜 Game Rules

The player can choose **Hit** to receive another card or **Stand** to stop taking cards. If the player's score goes above 21, the player loses (**Bust**). The dealer/computer automatically draws cards while its score is **less than 17** and stops at 17 or higher. If the dealer goes above 21, the player wins. If neither side busts, the player with the higher score wins. If both scores are equal, it is a draw.
